**Marisol Bay — El calor de la costa**: a top-down 2D open-world crime game in a fully original fictional city. Play as Milo Cruz, a getaway driver $40,000 in debt to fixer Vera Solano. Drive and steal cars, outrun the police across a 0–5 star wanted system (with helicopter response from 4 stars), fight the rival Tide Kings, and run 6 story missions — from a first delivery to robbing the Bay Bank. Living city with traffic, pedestrians, a minimap, HUD, synthesised WebAudio music and sound, and save/continue.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node --experimental-strip-types main.ts
```

## How to play

Open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser. **Goal:** pay off Milo's $40,000 debt by finishing all 6 story missions for Vera Solano — the last one is the Bay Bank job. **Controls:** WASD or arrow keys to move and drive, mouse to aim, hold left mouse button to shoot, E to enter or exit a car, Shift to run, Space for the handbrake while driving, P or Esc to pause, M to mute, C to continue a saved game, R to restart after getting busted or wasted. Watch the wanted stars: at 4 stars and above a police helicopter joins the chase, so break line of sight and lay low until the heat cools off. Progress saves automatically — continue from the title screen.
